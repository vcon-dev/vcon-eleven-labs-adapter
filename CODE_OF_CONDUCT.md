# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and community members, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Use welcoming language
- Be open to constructive criticism
- Focus on what's best for the community
- Show empathy toward others
- Ask questions before making assumptions

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination
- Offensive comments related to gender, identity, or beliefs
- Unwelcome advances or attention
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Threats or violent language
- Deliberate interruption or silencing

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying standards and taking action when behavior is unacceptable.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
1. Document the incident
2. Report to project maintainers
3. Include relevant details and context

### Consequences

- First violation: Warning and discussion
- Repeated violations: Temporary restrictions
- Serious violations: Permanent removal

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- Project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Community channels (chat, forums, events)
- Official project communications

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
